CurioByte

Short videos about AI tools, technology tips and tech discoveries.

CurioByte is a personal automation tool. It prepares short vertical videos about technology and uploads them to the CurioByte YouTube channel, which is owned and operated by the tool’s developer.

What the tool does

  • Finds a recent technology story from public news sources.
  • Writes a short script from that story and narrates it with a synthetic voice.
  • Renders a vertical video with captions.
  • Uploads the video to the developer’s own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Who can use it

Only the developer. CurioByte has no public sign-in, no accounts and no forms, and it never asks visitors for personal information. It connects to a single Google account, the developer’s own, to publish videos to that account’s YouTube channel.
